# Must not be said where people outside the organization read

A starting point. Copy it into the bot's repo and list it under
`streams.slack.screen.policy.external`, after the internal file, which still applies.

- Prices, rates, margins, budgets, and the terms of any contract or proposal, unless the
  people reading are the other party to it.
- Other clients or partners by name, and anything about their work.
- Plans, pipeline, staffing, and internal disagreements.
- Internal tools, systems, hostnames, repository names and links that only work inside.
