# Must not be said where the whole team reads

A starting point. Copy it into the bot's repo, make each item specific to what this bot holds,
and list it under `streams.slack.screen.policy.internal` and first under `policy.external`.

- Personal information about a person: health, family, money, home address, phone number,
  personal email, performance or pay, unless that person posted it in this conversation.
- What the operator told the bot in private, unless the operator said it may be shared.
- Credentials of any kind: tokens, passwords, keys, links that grant access by themselves.
